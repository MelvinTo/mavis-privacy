Mavis Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Mavis is an app for following and directing AI coding agents (such as Claude Code, Codex and opencode) that run under herdr on computers you own or control. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: Mavis collects nothing. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and its developer runs no servers that your information goes to. Mavis talks only to the servers you add, over SSH, and to Apple's iCloud if you keep sync on.

What stays on your device

  • The servers you add: host, port, username, and what signs you in to them (an SSH key the app makes, a key you paste, or a password), kept in the device's Keychain.
  • Conversations and images shown in the app, kept on the device so they open quickly, and the files and photos you are about to send.
  • A log of what the app did (connections, timings, and errors by kind; never your messages, keys or passwords). It leaves the device only if you share it yourself, from Settings → Share Log.

Photos, the camera and files are used only when you choose to attach one.

What goes to your servers

Everything Mavis sends goes over an encrypted SSH connection to the servers you added: the messages you type, the photos and files you attach (saved on that server, in ~/.cache/herdr-ios/uploads), and your answers to your agents' questions. From those servers Mavis reads your agents' conversations, their screens, and the files their replies name, to show them to you.

What your agents then do with your messages, including sending them to their AI providers, depends on how you set them up on your own computer and on those providers' terms, not on Mavis. Mavis can also ask an agent on your server to write short summaries and reports of your sessions; that agent runs on your computer, under your own account with its provider.

iCloud

With iCloud sync on (the default; you can turn it off in Settings), Mavis keeps your servers' settings, your session reports and the agents you have hidden in your iCloud key-value storage, and your servers' sign-in secrets in your iCloud Keychain, so that your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID have them. Apple stores this under your Apple ID; the developer of Mavis has no access to it.

On-device intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Mavis uses Apple's on-device model to choose an emoji for each agent from what it is working on. This happens on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere for it.

Children

Mavis is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone.
